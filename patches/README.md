# Patches to upstream open_health

Applied in order by the Dockerfile's build stage, right after checking out
`OPEN_HEALTH_REV`. A patch that no longer applies fails the build.

| Patch | Why |
|-------|-----|
| `dashboard-trim.patch` | Removes dashboard controls whose routes `bix-infra/nginx/health.conf` 404s on purpose: the Live heart rate panel (`/api/live-hr`), the DNA and Blood links (`/dna`, `/blood`), the ring auth key Export/Import buttons (`/api/ring-key`) and the tap-to-toggle on capabilities (`/api/feature`; the on/off states still show). Functions they called are left in place to keep the diff small. |
| `sleep-axis.patch` | The day page's Sleep lanes span only the night (start/end rounded out to whole hours, 1–3-hourly ticks) instead of the whole day; Activity and Heart rate keep the day axis. The shared cursor now moves in time, not axis fraction, so it still lines up across sections and hides where a moment is off a box's range. |
| `sleep-wake-rule.patch` | The signal-based sleep stager (used because Oura's models aren't available) scored any single motion burst as a 1.5-minute awakening, so rolling over counted as waking: ~25 "awakenings" a night. Now wake mid-night means movement in at least 6 of any 10 consecutive 30 s epochs (5 min), from the first moving epoch to the last. The ring logs a motion window only when you moved, so this separates getting up from turning over. Matt chose it 2026-10-15; untested against a known night-time trip. Adds two tests in `oura-summary`. |
| `sleep-window-and-wake.patch` | Fixes from the first two-ring night (2026-10-16/17, official Oura app on a second ring; see `../staging-eval/`). Ring 4 sends no hypnogram or `sleep_state`, so every night is signal-staged. (1) **Quiet wake:** the stager now reads `sleep_acm_period` (continuous accelerometer) and the ring's own `state_change` "activity" call; around each call, the chain of movements ≤ 7 min apart is wake (caught 02:58–03:38 lying still between fidgets; 5 min before). (2) **Onset** needs 10 min with no accelerometer movement (was 5 min of low `motion_event`, which smoothing then erased: latency was always 0). (3) **Window end:** an extension past the ring's declared bedtime end that the hypnogram scores all awake is trimmed back to the ring's end (sleep streams run until the next sync, which stretched a 07:17 wake-up to 10:13). (4) **Sleep debt:** an unstaged period under 1 h no longer voids its whole day. (5) The REM-latency sentence branches on short/normal/long. The 7-min link and 10-min onset were tuned on two nights only. REM and deep are unchanged and still unreliable (REM ≈ an 88-min template; deep capped at 10.5%). Adds five tests in `oura-summary`. |

To change one: check out the pin, `git apply` the patches, edit, then
`git diff > patches/<name>.patch` (paths relative to the open_health root).
